# AI-Enabled Cyber Operations
## A Threat Intelligence Case Study of AI-Orchestrated Cyber Espionage

An independent threat intelligence research project examining the reported use of AI in cyber espionage, with a focus on the GTG-1002 campaign disclosed by Anthropic in November 2025.

The investigation explores how AI capabilities can be integrated into cyber operations, how threat actor behaviour can be analysed, and what the implications are for security teams, detection and defence.

## Research Objectives

- Examine the GTG-1002 campaign and the activity documented in public reporting.
- Analyse the reported attack lifecycle and the role of AI throughout the operation.
- Identify relevant tactics, techniques and procedures (TTPs).
- Map documented activity to the MITRE ATT&CK framework.
- Assess the security implications of AI-enabled and AI-orchestrated operations.
- Develop practical recommendations for detection, mitigation and organisational resilience.

## Research Methodology

The project uses an evidence-based threat intelligence approach, combining:

- Open-source intelligence (OSINT) and primary-source research.
- Source evaluation and reliability assessment.
- Evidence tracking and confidence ratings.
- MITRE ATT&CK mapping.
- Analysis of AI-related attack capabilities and risks.
- Defensive analysis and intelligence assessment.

Documented facts, source assessments and analytical judgements will be clearly distinguished. Claims will not be treated as independently verified unless supported by appropriate evidence.

## Research Structure

The investigation will be organised into the following sections:

1. Executive Summary
2. Threat Actor Profile
3. Campaign Overview
4. Attack Lifecycle
5. MITRE ATT&CK Mapping
6. AI Security Analysis
7. Defensive Recommendations
8. Intelligence Assessment

Supporting materials will include an evidence register, source assessments, confidence matrix, diagrams and bibliography.

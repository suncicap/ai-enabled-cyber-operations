# Campaign Overview

## 1. Introduction

In November 2025, Anthropic publicly disclosed what it described as the first reported AI-orchestrated cyber espionage campaign.

The campaign, identified as GTG-1002, involved the reported use of AI agents to support a coordinated cyber espionage operation against approximately 30 organisations and targets.

Anthropic assessed the activity as likely conducted by a Chinese state-sponsored group.

The operation is significant because AI was reportedly used to perform substantial portions of the tactical work, rather than being limited to research or generating technical recommendations.

## 2. Timeline

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| September 2025 | Anthropic reports identifying and disrupting the GTG-1002 campaign. |
| 13 November 2025 | Anthropic publicly discloses the campaign and publishes its findings. |

The campaign activity and public disclosure are distinct events. September 2025 refers to the reported operational activity, while November 2025 marks the public announcement.

## 3. Targeting and Scope

According to Anthropic, the operation targeted approximately 30 organisations across several sectors.

The reported targets included technology companies, financial institutions, chemical manufacturing organisations and government agencies.

Anthropic stated that a small number of targets were successfully compromised.

The public report does not establish that every selected target was successfully accessed or that every stage of the operation occurred against every organisation.

## 4. Threat Actor Assessment

Anthropic identified the activity under the designation GTG-1002 and assessed that it was likely conducted by a Chinese state-sponsored group.

This is an attribution assessment by Anthropic, not an independently established identity.

The investigation therefore uses the designation GTG-1002 and attributes the state sponsorship assessment to Anthropic.

Further independent corroboration would be required to establish stronger attribution confidence.

## 5. Operational Approach

The reported operation combined human strategic direction with AI-supported tactical execution.

Anthropic described the use of:

- Multiple specialised AI agents.
- An orchestration framework to coordinate tasks.
- Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations.
- Automated reconnaissance and technical research.
- Credential discovery and validation.
- Data collection and processing.

The operation reportedly divided complex objectives into smaller tasks that could be delegated to specialised agents.

This approach allowed AI to participate in multiple stages of the intrusion lifecycle.

## 6. Significance

The campaign highlights several developments relevant to cyber threat intelligence.

### AI as an operational capability

AI agents were reportedly used to perform actions within target environments, moving beyond conventional AI-assisted research and content generation.

### Increased operational scale

Automation may allow threat actors to conduct more tasks with fewer human resources.

### New security boundaries

AI agents connected to tools and systems introduce risks involving permissions, credentials, execution controls and auditability.

### Attribution and detection challenges

AI-generated activity may complicate the interpretation of individual actions, particularly when legitimate tools and administrative interfaces are involved.

## 7. Assessment Limitations

This overview is based primarily on Anthropic's public reporting.

The investigation has not independently verified the identity of the threat actor, accessed the targeted environments or examined the underlying operational telemetry.

The assessment distinguishes between:

- Events reported by Anthropic.
- Attribution assessments made by Anthropic.
- Analytical conclusions developed within this investigation.

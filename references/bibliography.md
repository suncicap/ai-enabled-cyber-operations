# Bibliography and Sources

This bibliography lists the principal sources used in the analysis of the GTG-1002 AI-orchestrated cyber espionage campaign. Sources are distinguished by their role: incident reporting, threat-framework mapping, and AI-specific threat analysis.

## 1. Primary incident reporting

**Anthropic. (2025, November 13).** *Disrupting the first reported AI-orchestrated cyber espionage campaign.*

https://www.anthropic.com/news/disrupting-AI-espionage

Primary source for Anthropic's account of the GTG-1002 campaign, including the reported use of Claude, AI agents, reconnaissance, exploitation, credential collection, and other operational activities.

**Anthropic. (2025).** *Disrupting the first reported AI-orchestrated cyber espionage campaign* [Technical report].

https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/d7dd50dd1185f59be051b307150d877f2b82bd2c.pdf

Detailed technical report accompanying the public disclosure. Used to examine the reported campaign workflow, technical observations, and Anthropic's estimates of AI involvement.

**Source limitation:** Both publications originate from Anthropic and should be treated as one primary reporting source, not as independent corroboration.

## 2. MITRE ATT&CK

**MITRE.** *Campaign C0062.*

https://attack.mitre.org/campaigns/C0062/

Used as a structured reference for the campaign's documented adversary behaviour and its relationship to the MITRE ATT&CK knowledge base.

**MITRE.** *MITRE ATT&CK.*

https://attack.mitre.org/

Reference framework for categorising adversary tactics and techniques. Technique mappings in this project should be supported by incident evidence rather than inferred from framework descriptions alone.

## 3. AI-specific threat framework

**MITRE.** *MITRE ATLAS: Adversarial Threat Landscape for Artificial-Intelligence Systems.*

https://atlas.mitre.org/

Reference framework for analysing threats and attack techniques involving AI-enabled systems. Used to structure the AI security analysis and distinguish conventional cyber activity from risks specific to AI systems and workflows.

## 4. Methodological note

The analysis prioritises primary reporting and established threat frameworks. Anthropic's statements about the campaign are attributed to Anthropic and are not presented as independently verified facts.

MITRE ATT&CK and MITRE ATLAS provide classification frameworks; they are not independent witnesses to the campaign. Confidence assessments therefore reflect the available evidence and its limitations.
